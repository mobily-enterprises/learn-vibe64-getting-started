In this lesson you'll try a small, real application. You'll press a button,
read what comes back, and learn who to ask when you want help or a change.

It takes about 10–15 minutes. You don't need to know how to code. The
application lives in a **practice project** made just for you, so nothing you
do here can break anything that matters.

You can read each step on your own, or press **Teach me** at any time and
Colleague, your helper in Vibe64, will walk you through it. Press **Next step**
when you're ready to move on.
