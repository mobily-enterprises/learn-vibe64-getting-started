Vibe64 has a few places you'll use all the time:

- **The project** holds your application: its files, its history and its
  running copy. Your practice project is already open.
- **The coding conversation** is the chat where a coding agent works on your
  project and makes changes for you.
- **Preview** is where your application runs, so you can try it exactly as a
  visitor would.
- **Colleague** is your helper. It explains things, opens the right screens
  for you and can pass change requests on to the coding agent.

Press **Show me** to see where Preview is. On a phone, this lesson slides out
of the way while you look; open it again from the **L** button.

When you have found the project, its coding conversation and Preview, press
**Done**.
