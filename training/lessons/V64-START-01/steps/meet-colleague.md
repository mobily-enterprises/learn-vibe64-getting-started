Colleague is always nearby. Practise opening and closing it:

1. Open Colleague with its avatar button.
2. Type a question, or press **Talk** to speak. For example: “Where do I try
   the app?”
3. Use **Minimize conversation** to tuck it away. Your conversation is kept.
4. Press the avatar button again: the same conversation comes back.

While you work through a lesson, **Teach me** opens Colleague next to the
lesson so it can explain the current step and point at things on the screen.

When you can open, minimise and bring back Colleague, press **Done**.
