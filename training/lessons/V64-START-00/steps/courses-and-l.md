Two buttons help you move around:

- **Courses**, at the right end of the yellow step bar (a book icon on a
  phone), opens the list of lessons. Its tabs are **Courses**, **In
  progress** and **Archived**. Close it with × or Escape.
- **L**, at the top left, switches between Learn and your own projects. Press
  **Show me** to see it. Its tooltip says **Back to Develop (your projects)**
  in Learn mode and **Switch to Learn** in Develop.

Try both: open Courses and close it, then press **L**, and press **L** again
to come back here.

When you're back on this step, press **Done**.
