Two buttons help you move around:

- **Courses**, at the right end of the yellow step bar (a book icon on a
  phone), opens the list of lessons. Its tabs are **Courses**, **In
  progress** and **Archived**. Close it with × or Escape.
- **L**, at the top left, switches between Learn and your own projects. Its
  tooltip says **Back to Develop (your projects)** in Learn mode and
  **Switch to Learn** in Develop.

Try both: open Courses and close it, then press **L**, and press **L** again
to come back. On a phone you come back to the coding chat: press **Show
project** to see this step again.

When you're back on this step, the step bar marks it done (or press
**Done**).

```beats
do courses: Open Courses, look around, then close it with × or Escape.
do learning: Now press L to visit your own projects, then L again to come back here.
check done: Back on this step? Press Done.
```
