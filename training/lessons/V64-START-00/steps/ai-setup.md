Vibe64 doesn't come with an AI of its own: you connect the AI service you want
to use. The coding agent and Colleague both use it.

- You connect it in the AI setup, which is always in **Account settings →
  AI Accounts**. While no AI is connected, Vibe64 also points you there, for
  example from the coding chat's send button.
- On a shared workspace, only its owner connects AI.
- An AI key works like a password. Never paste it into a chat, a lesson or a
  project file.
