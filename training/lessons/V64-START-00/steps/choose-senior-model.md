Connecting a key and choosing a model are two separate things. Now choose
which model future **Senior** coding chats use.

1. In **AI Accounts**, open **Configure routing** on the OpenCode card.
2. For **Senior**, choose **GLM-4.7** if it's offered.
3. Review your choice and press **Save routing**.

After connecting, Vibe64 may instead show **Current** and **Suggested** choices
with an **Apply** button. Check every change it suggests; **Keep current
routing** leaves everything as it is.

Things to know:

- If GLM-4.7 is locked, **Unlock all Z.AI models** explains the paid credit
  it needs. You choose **Keep free only** or **Unlock paid models**.
- If you'd rather stay free, you can choose **GLM-4.7-Flash** instead. That's
  your choice to make.
- The **Default** badge you may see after connecting means GLM-4.7-Flash is the
  preferred connection, not that GLM-4.7 was chosen.
- Changing Senior changes only future Senior chats. **Junior** chats and
  **Colleague** keep their own model choices, and a chat that's already
  running keeps its model.

Press **Done** when your routing is saved, or when you've decided to leave it
for later.
