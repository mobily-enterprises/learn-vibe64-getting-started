**Preview** is where your app runs. You see it exactly as a visitor would.

1. Select **Preview**. **Do it for me** in the step bar opens it for you.
2. Your app says **My first Vibe64 app** and has a blue box.

While Preview is showing, a small toolbar appears next to the switches at the
top. Two of its buttons have the same circular-arrow icon, so go by their
tooltips:

- **Reload preview** loads the page again.
- **Restart preview** starts the whole app again.

On a phone, press **Show preview controls** to see them.

Once you're in Preview, the step bar marks this step done (or press
**Done**).

```beats
do preview: Select Preview to see your app, running.
show preview: It says My first Vibe64 app and has a blue box. A visitor sees exactly this.
show reload-preview: Reload preview loads the page again. Restart preview, beside it, starts the whole app again.
check done: Seen your app? Press Done.
```
