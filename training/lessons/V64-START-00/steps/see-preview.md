**Preview** is where your app runs. You see it exactly as a visitor would.

1. Select **Preview**, or press **Show me**.
2. Your app says **My first Vibe64 app** and has a blue box.

Above the app there's a small toolbar. Two of its buttons have the same
circular-arrow icon, so go by their tooltips:

- **Reload preview** loads the page again.
- **Restart preview** starts the whole app again.

On a phone, press **Show preview controls** to see them.

Then select **Lesson** again (or press **Open** in the step bar) and press
**Done**.
