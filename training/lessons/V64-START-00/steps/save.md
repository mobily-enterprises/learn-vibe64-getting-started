A change isn't kept for good until you **save** it.

1. Press the **Save** button (a disk icon) above the coding chat.
2. The **Save changes** dialog says how many files changed. **View diff**
   shows them. Press the save button at the bottom of the dialog.
3. Open **Dashboard → Git log**, or press **Show me**. Your saved version is
   at the top of the list.

No change yet? Then there's nothing to save, and Git log shows only your
project's first version.

When your saved version is in Git log, press **Done**.
