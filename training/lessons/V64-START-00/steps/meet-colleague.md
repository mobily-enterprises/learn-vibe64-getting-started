Colleague is your teacher and helper. Let's practise:

1. Press the avatar button at the top right to open Colleague.
2. Type a question, or press **Talk** to speak. Try: “What is the Dashboard
   for?”
3. Press **Minimize conversation** to tuck Colleague away. Your conversation
   is kept.
4. Press the avatar button again: the same conversation comes back.

While a lesson is going, Colleague's header has a yellow **Step** button
(like **Step 11/16**). It shows the step you're on in the Lesson pane.

Colleague explains, answers questions and can open screens for you. It never
marks your steps. Colleague needs an AI too: if none is connected yet, it
says so, and you can still practise opening and closing it.

On a phone Colleague fills the screen. Its **Step** button or **Minimize
conversation** brings the lesson back.

When you've opened and minimised Colleague, the step bar marks this step
done (or press **Done**). Then bring it back once to see your conversation.
