A few ideas before you start:

- **Z.AI supplies the model; Vibe64 is where you use it.** An **API key** lets
  Vibe64 use your Z.AI account. Treat it like a password: never paste it into
  a chat, a lesson answer, a screenshot or a project file.
- **GLM-4.7** is the model we recommend for coding. It uses paid credit on
  your Z.AI account.
- **GLM-4.7-Flash** is a different, smaller model. It is currently listed as
  free. It is not the same as GLM-4.7.
- Creating an account or a key doesn't by itself give you paid models. Check
  [Z.AI's current pricing](https://docs.z.ai/guides/overview/pricing) before
  you decide to pay; prices can change.
- This lesson uses the **regular Z.AI API**. A **Coding Plan** is a separate
  Z.AI subscription with its own setup; you don't need it here.

Whether to pay, and for what, is always your decision.
