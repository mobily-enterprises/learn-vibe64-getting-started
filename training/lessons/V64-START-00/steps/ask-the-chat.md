Now ask the coding agent for a tiny change.

1. Click the message box at the bottom of the coding chat. On a phone, press
   **Show chat** first.
2. Type a request in your own words, for example:
   “Please change the heading to say Hello from Sam and make the box green.”
   (Use your own name.) **Do it for me** in the step bar sends one for you.
3. Press the send button (the arrow) or Enter.
4. Wait while the agent works. This can take a few minutes. When it has
   finished, it tells you what it changed. If you change your mind, **Stop**
   stops it.

**Your AI not answering?** If the send button says **Connect your AI account
to send messages**, your AI needs connecting again: the step **Where AI setup
lives** says where. You can also skip this step and the next one: the rest of
the lesson still works.

When the agent says it has finished, the step bar marks this step done (or
press **Done**).
