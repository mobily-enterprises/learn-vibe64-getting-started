You're reading the **Lesson** pane. Three things help you find your way:

- **The step list** shows every step and its status: **Passed**, **Not yet
  passed**, **Not checked yet** or **Optional**. When there's little room it
  becomes a row of numbered circles. Select a step to read it.
- **Next step** and **Previous step** move through the lesson. **Lesson
  notes** has extra background whenever you want it.
- **The yellow step bar** above the page always shows where you are
  (**Step 3 of 16**). Its arrows change step, **Open** brings this Lesson pane
  back from anywhere, and **Courses** at its right end lists all the lessons.
  The small arrow at its left folds it to one line.

A step is marked in one of three ways: an automatic check, your answer to a
question, or your own **Done**. Nobody marks steps for you, not even
Colleague.
