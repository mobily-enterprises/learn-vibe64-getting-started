You're reading the **Lesson** pane. Three things help you find your way:

- **The step list** shows every step. A green tick means the step is done;
  **Optional** steps can be skipped. When there's little room, the list
  becomes a row of numbered circles. Select a step to read it.
- **Next step** and **Previous step** move through the lesson. **Lesson
  notes** has extra background whenever you want it.
- **The yellow step bar** above the page always shows where you are
  (**3/16**, with a dot for each of the step's lines). Its arrows move line by
  line and into the next step, **Step** opens the whole step text right under
  it, and **Courses** at its right end lists all the lessons. The **Lesson**
  tab brings this Lesson pane back from anywhere.

A step is marked by an automatic check, your answer to a question, a review
of your work by your own AI, or your own **Done**. When a step only asks you
to do things, the bar marks it done as soon as it has seen you do them all.
Colleague never marks steps for you.

```beats
show lesson: This is the Lesson pane: the step list, and the text of the step you're on.
show lesson-bar: The bar shows where you are. Its arrows move one line at a time. Step opens the whole step.
show courses: Courses lists all the lessons.
```
