Go back to **Dashboard → Database**. The screen still shows the database as it
was before your change, so press **Refresh schema** first.

Now open **ERD**. Your change probably added something to the `notes` table,
such as a `done` column. Open **Data** to see which notes are ticked.

When you want to know what an app *really* saved, the database is the place to
look.
