1. Open **Preview**.
2. Type a note in the **New note** box, for example “Call the dentist”.
3. Choose a notebook, such as **Home** or **Work**.
4. Press **Add note**.

The page says **Saved.** and your note appears in the list with its notebook.
Reload the page in Preview: your note is still there.

The page asked the server to save your note, and the server stored it in the
project's **database**: a place built for keeping data safely, even when the
app restarts.

Press **Done** when you've added a note.
