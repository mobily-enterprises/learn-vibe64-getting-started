# Good to know: Welcome to Vibe64

These notes add background to the tour. You can read them at any time.

## What the tour shows

The tour runs on the screens outside your projects. It has no practice project
and changes nothing in your work: it points at each place and, when a line
asks you to open one, notices when you have. **Do it for me** opens it for you.

Some installations have more screens than others, so the tour shows only the
steps that exist here and that are open to you.

## Taking it again

**Take the tour** in the account menu starts it again at any time. If another
lesson is in progress, Vibe64 asks before archiving it.
