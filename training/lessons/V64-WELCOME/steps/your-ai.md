Vibe64 doesn't come with an AI of its own: it uses the AI service you connect.
The coding agent that builds your apps and Colleague both use it.

- AI setup is in the **account menu** at the top right: **Redo onboarding**
  lets you choose or switch your AI at any time.
- On a shared workspace, the owner sets up the AI for everyone.
- An AI key works like a password. Never paste it into a chat, a lesson or a
  project file.

Press **Check now** to confirm your AI is ready. If it isn't yet, set it up,
then check again.

```beats
show account-menu: Your account menu. AI setup, Take the tour and your settings live here.
show ai-setup: Redo onboarding opens AI setup, to choose or switch your AI.
check fact: Press Check now to confirm your AI is ready.
```
