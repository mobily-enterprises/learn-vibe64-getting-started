**L** switches between two modes:

- **Learn**, where lessons run, each with its own practice project when it
  needs one. The headers turn yellow.
- **Develop**, your own projects. A lesson waits while you're there, and **L**
  brings you back to the same step.

Try it: press **L** to go to Develop, then press **L** again to come back here.
