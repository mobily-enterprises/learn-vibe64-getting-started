Every app you build lives in a **project**. Click a project to open it in the
editor, where you chat with the AI, try it in Preview and save your work.

While you're learning, this list shows only your lesson's practice project.
Press **L** to see your own projects again. The workspace owner adds new
projects there with **Add project**, from a starter or from a GitHub
repository.

```beats
do manage-projects: Open Projects.
show manage-projects: Every app you build lives in a project. Click one to open it in the editor.
```
