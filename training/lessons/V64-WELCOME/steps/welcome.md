Welcome to Vibe64! This short tour shows you the screens around your projects:
where your AI is set up, who helps you, and how lessons work. It takes about
five minutes and changes nothing in your work.

Vibe64 works like this: **you describe what you want, an AI coding agent
builds it, and you try the result.**

- **The yellow step bar** walks you through each step, one short line at a
  time, pointing at the real screen. When a line asks you to do something, it
  notices when you've done it.
- **Lesson** on the bar shows the whole tour, at the step you're on.
- **Teach me** asks Colleague to explain the step you're on.

```beats
show lesson-bar: Hello! This yellow bar walks you through the tour, one short line at a time.
show lesson: The Lesson shows the whole tour, at the step you're on, whenever you want to read it.
```
