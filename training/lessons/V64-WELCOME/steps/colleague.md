**Colleague** is your helper on every screen. Ask it how to do something, or
ask it to do it for you. During a lesson it is your teacher: it explains, and
it can point at things on the real screen, but it never marks your steps.

Open Colleague with the avatar button. Try asking: "What can you help me
with?" Your conversation is kept when you close it.
